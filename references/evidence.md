# Research and policy record

This skill uses the supplied writing exercises as its foundation and independently develops a strict editorial policy. No other writing skill supplies instructions or assets. The papers below informed the catalogue. They do not establish that a construction uniquely identifies AI, that every model uses it, or that removing it always improves writing. “Eliminating formulaic AI prose” describes an editing objective.

## Editorial policy versus empirical findings

The absolute bans on negative redefinition, additive escalation, rhetorical parallelism, and rhetorical questions express this project's chosen style. Human writers also use those devices effectively. The policy intentionally excludes them from original deliverables; the evidence does not make them intrinsically wrong.

P05, P06, P12, P14, and P22 also operationalize the supplied draft's requirements for substance, support, and non-repetition. Specific examples in the catalogue are original illustrations, not quotations from studies. They should never be described as measured high-frequency strings unless a later study supplies that measurement.

No single-source statistics are reproduced as universal prevalence claims. No authorship detector, “human score,” or evasion claim serves as a success metric. Compare source fidelity, substantive progress, readability, and compliance on the supplied task.
