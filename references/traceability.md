## Operational interpretations

These choices translate the source guidance and user requirements into editorial decisions. They describe the implementation; they do not establish separate user approval of every rule.

| Choice | Basis and boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Continuous prose for essays and the course assignment | The essay instructions remove AI-generated headings and lists. Reports and memos may retain requested or genre-required informative structure under R08. |
| Substantive questions may guide an argument | The screenshots recommend question-led sections and open-ended inquiry. P04 removes empty question-and-answer staging. |
| Parallel and cumulative clauses may develop distinct reasoning | The paragraph exercise explicitly permits these devices. P03 removes ceremonial symmetry, duplicate claims and forced triads. |
| Revision uses supplied materials by default | R02 prevents invented additions. Explicitly authorized research may supply verified, attributed evidence; C12 still governs outside sources in the course assignment. |
| Exact quotations retain their wording | R15 protects evidence and attribution. Fabricating a quotation cannot create an exception. |
| Meaning takes priority over cosmetic variation | R03 preserves authorial judgments even when their support needs clarification. A missing warrant alone cannot justify deleting a substantive position. |
| Criticism and endings depend on the task | R06 permits criticism after fair reconstruction outside the course restriction. R13 follows the result established by the argument; C05 and C10 retain the course's open inquiry. |
| Grammar rules target identified uses | P07–P11 distinguish problematic framing from ordinary grammatical forms. A word alone cannot establish AI authorship. |

## Resolving conflicts

Apply the task's explicit instructions and host instruction hierarchy. Load course restrictions only for the identified assignment. Interpret the broad request to remove formulaic writing alongside the source's permitted techniques: assess what a question or parallel clause contributes before changing it. Preserve the explicit ban on negative redefinition.

Protect meaning, exact evidence and attribution. Explain a consequential conflict when a faithful revision cannot satisfy a mandatory constraint. A clarification may ask for the missing evidence or intended claim. Do not attribute an editorial interpretation to the user without supporting instructions.

## Operational scope

The workflow makes the requirements actionable through task routing, voice-sample boundaries, paragraph planning and source-to-output review. Each showcased revision exposes its complete factual input. Each pattern supplies a worked repair or a missing-evidence response. These authored examples specify intended behavior; recorded model runs provide separate evidence of execution.
