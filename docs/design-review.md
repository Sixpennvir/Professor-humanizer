# Design review: 2026-10-14

## Example design

Each example displays the complete draft and its revision so readers can check the source of every factual claim. The homepage demonstrates paragraph-level editing through a close reading; the full-length example follows an argument through its objection and conditional conclusion.

## Independent decisions

The homepage now demonstrates close reading because academic analysis anchors the supplied brief. The examples distinguish an interpretation from its source evidence and make each paragraph's contribution visible.

Each showcased revision comes from a complete draft. Notes specify the task and provenance; they supply no facts missing from the draft. Automatic checks protect selected literal anchors for quotations and facts. Interpretation and completeness require editorial review.

The strict profile preserves substantive questions and parallel clauses that develop distinct reasoning. It removes empty staging and repetitive symmetry. Essay structure follows the course's continuous-prose guidance; reports and memos may retain requested or genre-required organization. Familiar grammar remains subject to use-specific boundaries.

The skill distinguishes revision from drafting and diagnosis. Revision uses supplied material by default; explicitly authorized research can add verified, attributed sources within the task's evidence boundary. The course profile keeps its instructor-approval requirement for outside sources. Author samples guide expression and contribute no evidence to the target.

A judgment with a missing warrant still requires preservation of its substance. Examples and evaluation expectations must distinguish that judgment from empty framing. The agent identifies a support gap before making any consequential change to the author's position. Clear prose can remain unchanged.

## Acceptance criteria

- A reader can compare every homepage fact directly with the displayed draft.
- The academic example demonstrates a warranted inference and a substantive second paragraph.
- Every prohibited family includes a complete input and a concrete repair or an explicit clarification.
- Generated documentation cannot silently drift from its example data.
- Source instructions, explicit user requirements and operational interpretations remain distinguishable in traceability.
- Each substantive claim, qualification and objection survives a revision or appears in an explicit clarification.
- Long-form review checks non-adjacent paragraphs for repeated conclusions and lost reasoning.
- Checks report their actual scope; no fabricated model evaluation appears.
- Release assets match the verified source commit and include the required runtime references.
